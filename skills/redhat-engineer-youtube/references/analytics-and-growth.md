# Choosing topics and titles

The operator posts each video by hand, so the pipeline has no view counts, retention curves or subscriber data, and the agent must never invent any.

What the agent can do to make the channel improve:

- Choose topics per `topic-strategy.md`: balance the five series, prefer stories with a strong visual and a surprising consequence, and about one in four evergreen "how X works" topics.
- Write titles and thumbnail text from the formulas in `thumbnail-and-metadata.md`, and change one variable at a time when trying something new.
- Log every deliberate change in `reports/changelog.md`, and any experiment the operator wants tracked in `reports/experiments.md`.
- If the operator leaves notes about how a video performed, take them into account when picking the next topic and titles.
- Resist format drift for its own sake: the identity is the asset. Improve inside the format, change the format only with evidence.
